Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how North Lane Labs collects, uses, and protects information for iOS apps we publish.

Information We Collect

Our apps may collect app usage information, onboarding responses, saved content, device/app diagnostics, subscription status, and account identifiers needed to provide app functionality.

Subscriptions and Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Subscription status may be managed through RevenueCat so the app can verify active entitlements and restore purchases.

AI Features

Some apps may include AI-powered features. When you use those features, your prompts and related app context may be sent through a server-side gateway to configured AI providers so the app can generate the requested output. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless the app clearly asks for it and you intend to provide it.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate app features, sync user preferences, manage subscriptions, enforce usage limits, improve reliability, provide support, and maintain app security.

Data Sharing

We may use service providers for payments, analytics, crash reporting, backend infrastructure, and AI processing. We do not sell personal information.

Account and Data Deletion

Where an app creates an account or persistent profile, the app will provide a way to request account or data deletion from inside the app or through support.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact hello@northlanelabs.com.
